# T-Cube website design

The user requests a modern, visually continuous page: no black-only surface, no alternation between dark and light sections, and fewer unnecessary labels. Chinese is the default, with English available.

## Visual system

A continuous low-saturation blue-grey ground (`#192938`) runs from the sticky header through the footer. Section backgrounds are transparent. Hierarchy comes from type, a consistent grid, spacing and fine separators. Product stages use translucent shades from the same blue-grey family; no white panels, alternating bands or saturated CTA chapters are introduced.

Self-hosted Manrope accompanies native Chinese sans fonts. The hero leads with the actual product name, T-Cube, and a short factual description. Other headings name capabilities directly. Mint is reserved for links, selected material controls and downloads. Body text has a measured line length and high contrast.

## Composition

The six-face launcher story and material preview share one section, reducing repeated cube renders. Desktop controls, clipboard history, local data, complete features and downloads follow in one visual world. The duplicate screenshot gallery is removed; real product imagery remains in the relevant feature passages. Full functionality and honest platform/privacy/install requirements remain in structured details and FAQ.

## Motion and access

The cube floats slowly, with restrained refracted ribbons and a pointer-responsive light field fading into the shared ground. No orbit rings, stars, particle rain or unrelated counters. Effects run automatically while the product stage is visible; loops stop offscreen/hidden, and reduced-motion yields a static stage. Desktop/compact canvas caps remain 30/20fps with bounded pixel density. The header has no motion control, as explicitly requested by the maintainer. Mobile controls retain 44px touch targets.

All content and download anchors remain available without JavaScript. Chinese fallback text is regenerated from canonical translations rather than modified with substring replacements. Material tabs, native dialogs, keyboard focus and live release metadata remain functional.

## Responsive validation

Validate Chinese/English at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels, plus motion controls and reduced motion. Use actual rendered screenshots, not generic detector results, to judge visual consistency.
