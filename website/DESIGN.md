# T-Cube website design

The user's pinned brief wins: technology-forward, premium product storytelling informed by Apple and OpenAI, Chinese by default with English available. The autonomous implementation uses native product evidence rather than AI-generated UI mockups; no permanent build-path preference is inferred.

Direction: an optical product exhibition. Black graphite surfaces, cool white type, restrained ice-cyan links, large real native renders, and alternating open editorial passages and bounded product stages. The hero is a typographic product title beside a large lit cube crossing the composition, with the primary download action inside the first viewport. Lower sections change scale and composition: horizontal launcher story; interactive material evidence; full-width desktop control stage; clipboard close-up; quiet privacy typography; complete feature index; platform downloads. No invented testimonials, benchmark numbers or simulated product interfaces.

The considered visual systems were an optical exhibition, industrial product catalog, contemporary software release page, architectural photography monograph, scientific instrument exhibition (candidate 5, selected), typographic Swiss product poster, and a digital sculpture gallery. Candidate 5 fits the pinned user brief through actual product scale, measured white space, labels grounded in functionality, and restrained cyan interaction. The concept seed seed:3e5538ac39072163 was consulted; cartoon, distressed collage and bitmap-scroller challengers conflict with the explicit premium technology brief and readability of the complete function reference. Their useful discipline is a distinctive silhouette, varied editorial scale and clear information depth, not borrowed decoration.

Typography: self-hosted Manrope Latin, native Chinese sans fallback. White display type with no gradient text. Body 16–18px, visible secondary copy, maximum display 96px. No decorative kickers or arbitrary section numbers.

Motion: one slow hero reveal / pointer response, material changes only on explicit activation; content never hidden for JS entrances. Native motion preview opt-in. prefers-reduced-motion stops decorative motion and selects static media. All interactions work by keyboard, focus is visible, language controls retain selection, gallery opens in a native accessible dialog.

Responsive: 1280px content maximum; 1440 / 1024 / 768 / 390 / 320 widths checked. Mobile uses full-width imagery and stacked prose, with no tiny faux desktop wrappers. Download links remain native anchors without JavaScript.
